Grace Period Policies

What happens when Day 30 ends? expand_more

On Day 31 (Dec 16, 2025), tenant write-access is locked. Under the Sri Lankan Data Protection standard, encrypted databases are held in cold storage for 60 additional days before any decommissioning occurs.

Can students log in during grace? expand_more

Yes. Students retain read-only access to view their recorded grades and previous marks matrices. High-bandwidth live streaming and assignment submissions are paused until active renewal.
