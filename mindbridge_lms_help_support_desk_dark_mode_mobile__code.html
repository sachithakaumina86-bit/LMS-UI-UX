Frequently Asked Questions

My bank deposit slip is still under review expand_more
Bank slip verification takes between 2 to 6 hours during bank operational hours. Please ensure the reference number, student registration ID, and the cashier branch stamp are crystal clear in the photo upload.
Can't play 1080p recorded video lecture expand_more
Adaptive streaming requires minimum 5 Mbps stable connection. Try toggling down to 720p or 480p from the gear icon inside the Combined Maths video player dashboard.
Physical gate NFC turnstile pass not scanning expand_more
Ensure screen brightness is above 80% if presenting digital QR, or present your physical plastic smartcard to the support counter at Apex Nugegoda or Rotary Bambalapitiya for instant re-magnetization.
How to update registered WhatsApp phone number expand_more
For security against unauthorized tutorial sharing, phone number revisions require SMS OTP verification on both old and new numbers via Profile > Account Security.
